Progressive Overload Phases

Phase 1
Foundation
Weeks 1-4
Master form on all movements. Start with weights at the higher end of rep ranges. Focus on mind-muscle connection and controlled movements.
Phase 2
Build
Weeks 5-8
Increase weight by 2.5-5% when you hit all reps at the top of the range. Push for progressive overload every session.
Phase 3
Push
Weeks 9-12
Work at the lower rep ranges with heavier weights. Add an extra set to compounds if recovery allows. Then deload and restart.
