## WORKOUT A - Push Focus

**Total Time:** ~50-55 minutes (including warm-up)

### Warm-Up (5 minutes)
- 2 min: Light cardio (jumping jacks, high knees)
- 3 min: Dynamic stretches (arm circles, leg swings, bodyweight squats)

### Main Workout

| # | Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Equipment |
|---|----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| 1 | **Smith Machine Squat** | 4 x 8-10 | 90 sec | Smith Machine |
| 2 | **Smith Machine Bench Press** | 4 x 8-10 | 90 sec | Smith Machine + Bench |
| 3 | **Cable Chest Fly** | 3 x 12 | 60 sec | Dual Pulleys |
| 4 | **Dumbbell Shoulder Press** | 3 x 10-12 | 60 sec | Dumbbells |
| 5 | **Tricep Rope Pushdown** | 3 x 12-15 | 60 sec | Cable + Rope |
| 6 | **Kettlebell Goblet Squat** | 3 x 12 | 60 sec | Kettlebell |

### Exercise Execution Guide

**1. Smith Machine Squat**
- Feet shoulder-width, slightly forward of the bar
- Descend until thighs parallel to floor
- Drive through heels, squeeze glutes at top
- *Target: Quads, Glutes, Core*

**2. Smith Machine Bench Press**
- Position bench so bar travels to mid-chest
- Lower with control, press explosively
- Keep shoulder blades pinched together
- *Target: Chest, Front Delts, Triceps*

**3. Cable Chest Fly**
- Set pulleys at shoulder height
- Slight bend in elbows throughout
- Squeeze chest hard at the center
- *Target: Chest (stretch and squeeze)*

**4. Dumbbell Shoulder Press**
- Seated or standing
- Press straight up, don't flare elbows excessively
- Control the descent
- *Target: Shoulders, Triceps*

**5. Tricep Rope Pushdown**
- Keep elbows pinned to sides
- Pull rope apart at bottom for peak contraction
- *Target: Triceps*

**6. Kettlebell Goblet Squat**
- Hold kettlebell at chest
- Deep squat, elbows inside knees
- Great finisher for legs and core
- *Target: Quads, Glutes, Core*

## WORKOUT B - Pull Focus

**Total Time:** ~50-55 minutes (including warm-up)

### Warm-Up (5 minutes)
- 2 min: Light cardio
- 3 min: Band pull-aparts, cat-cow stretches, hip circles

### Main Workout

| # | Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | Equipment |
|---|----------|-------------|------|-----------|
| 1 | **Smith Machine Romanian Deadlift** | 4 x 8-10 | 90 sec | Smith Machine |
| 2 | **Pull-Ups / Assisted Pull-Ups** | 4 x 6-10 | 90 sec | Multi-Grip Bar |
| 3 | **Cable Row (Close Grip Triangle)** | 4 x 10-12 | 60 sec | Cable + Triangle |
| 4 | **Dumbbell Bent Over Row** | 3 x 10 each arm | 60 sec | Dumbbells |
| 5 | **Cable Bicep Curl (Straight Bar)** | 3 x 12-15 | 60 sec | Cable + Straight Bar |
| 6 | **Face Pulls** | 3 x 15 | 60 sec | Cable + Rope |

### Exercise Execution Guide

**1. Smith Machine Romanian Deadlift**
- Slight knee bend, hinge at hips
- Feel deep hamstring stretch
- Squeeze glutes at top
- *Target: Hamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back*

**2. Pull-Ups**
- Use multi-grip bar (wider = more lats, narrow = more biceps)
- Full extension at bottom, chin over bar at top
- If needed: use resistance band for assistance
- *Target: Lats, Biceps, Rear Delts*

**3. Cable Row (Close Grip)**
- Sit tall, pull to lower chest
- Squeeze shoulder blades together
- Control the negative
- *Target: Mid Back, Lats, Biceps*

**4. Dumbbell Bent Over Row**
- Support on bench or free-standing
- Pull elbow back, squeeze at top
- *Target: Lats, Rhomboids, Biceps*

**5. Cable Bicep Curl**
- Keep elbows stationary
- Full range of motion
- Squeeze at top, slow negative
- *Target: Biceps*

**6. Face Pulls**
- High pulley position
- Pull to face, externally rotate at end
- Essential for shoulder health
- *Target: Rear Delts, Rotator Cuff*

## Progressive Overload Plan

This is HOW you build muscle. Follow this 12-week cycle:

### Weeks 1-4: Foundation
- Start with weights you can handle for the higher end of rep ranges
- Focus on perfect form
- Get comfortable with all movements

### Weeks 5-8: Build
- Increase weight by 2.5-5% when you can complete all sets at the top of the rep range
- Example: If prescribed 4x8-10, when you hit 4x10, add weight next session

### Weeks 9-12: Push
- Aim for the lower rep ranges with heavier weight
- Add an extra set to compound movements if recovery allows

### Then Reset
After 12 weeks, take a deload week (reduce weight by 40%, same reps), then start a new 12-week block with your new baseline.

## FAQ

**Q: What if I miss a workout?**
A: Don't double up. Just continue with the next scheduled workout.

**Q: When should I increase weight?**
A: When you can complete all prescribed sets at the TOP of the rep range with good form.

**Q: I'm not gaining weight?**
A: Eat more. Add 200 calories. Ectomorphs need to be aggressive with food.

**Q: Can I add cardio?**
A: Light cardio (walking) is fine. Avoid excessive cardio as it burns calories you need for muscle growth.

**Q: How do I know it's working?**
A: Strength going up + weight going up + looking better in photos = working.
