> **Compliance note:** Australia's Spam Act 2003 requires consent to send commercial electronic messages. A purchased/scraped list is high-risk for cold email. Safer route: run this as content to a list you've built with consent, or as paid ads / LinkedIn outreach where cold contact is permitted. Worth a quick check with your marketing/legal contact before a bulk cold send.

## Notes & variations
- **Newsletter logic:** The email's only job is the click. One hook, one link, minimal friction. Every "Book a call" decision happens on the landing page, where you can show all four options, social proof, and an FAQ without overwhelming the inbox.
- **One link, repeated:** Use the same landing-page URL for the button, the P.S., and the plain-text fallback. Don't scatter different destinations — it dilutes the click and muddies your analytics.
- **Track it:** Add UTM params to `[LANDING_PAGE_URL]` so Beehiiv click data maps to landing-page conversions. Suggested: `?utm_source=beehiiv&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=budget2026&utm_content=v1`.
- **Tone:** Deliberately provocative + empathetic. The budget hook earns attention; the promise of four concrete options earns the click.
- **Softer subject (deliverability):** If cold sending, lead with subject #5 — it's less likely to trip spam filters than "destroyed."
- **Follow-up #2 (3 days later):** Short bump to non-clickers — "Did the budget change your numbers? Whether you want to keep going or get out cleanly, we have a path." Same landing-page link.
- **Landing page:** Built as `landing-page.html` (see below) — open it in a browser to preview. Host it anywhere (Beehiiv page, Carrd, Webflow, a static host) and point `[LANDING_PAGE_URL]` at it.

**Who this is for:** Licensed buyer's agents whose acquisition pipeline depends on negatively geared investment property, now under pressure from the 2026 federal budget.

**Your four pathways**

| Pathway | What it is | Best for | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | We buy your business (entity/assets) outright | Owners ready for a clean exit | Upfront value + [earnout / transition terms] |
| **Merger** | Combine your operation into ours | Operators who want to keep working | Equity / role in the combined business + shared overhead |

**Valuation basis (talking points — set your real numbers):**
- Recurring revenue / trail and active client count
- Pipeline and settled-deal history (last 12–24 months)
- Database size, quality, and consent status
- Brand, team, and any rent-roll / referral agreements

**Indicative process:**
1. Confidential 15-min call → 2. NDA + high-level numbers → 3. Indicative offer → 4. Light due diligence → 5. Terms & completion. Typical timeframe: [X weeks].

**Client-data handling (do this right):**
- Transferring or selling a client list is regulated under the Privacy Act 1988 (APPs). Personal information generally can't be disclosed for a purpose the client wasn't told about without consent or an applicable exception.
- Confirm what the seller's privacy policy / collection notices allowed, and plan consent or notification to clients on transfer.
- Get this reviewed by a privacy/legal adviser before any list changes hands — this protects both sides and the clients. *(General information, not legal advice.)*
